Privacy policy

This policy covers the Yapgrab browser extension ("Yapgrab"), called Blurt before version 0.2.0.

Summary

Yapgrab does not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal data. Transcripts and your viewing activity stay in your browser.

Information Yapgrab processes

Captions of the video you are watching. When you click Yapgrab, the extension requests the captions of the current video from the video site, from your browser and with your existing session, and formats them on your device. This text is not sent to us or to anyone else. You decide where it goes by copying it or saving it as a file.

Captions of several videos you choose. When you use "Export several videos" with your open tabs, a playlist or a channel, Yapgrab does the same for each video you pick, one at a time with pauses in between and only while its side panel is open, and puts the results in one ZIP file that you save. Nothing is sent to us.

Information stored on your device. Yapgrab keeps the following in your browser's extension storage: your settings, a count of successful uses (to show a one-time waitlist note), whether you dismissed that note, your choice for the optional failure counter, and a short local log of recent extraction attempts (time, video id, which step failed). When you export several videos, it also keeps that export (the videos you picked, their status and transcripts) until you start a new one, and an export history (video id, title and date of each video exported this way) so it can skip videos you already exported; "Forget export history" in the side panel clears it. This information never leaves your device unless you export the log and send it to us yourself. Removing the extension deletes it.

Information sent over the network

To the video site. Requests for captions go to the video site, as they would when you use its own transcript feature. When you export several videos, Yapgrab also loads the playlist or channel pages you picked and the page of each chosen video. The site's own privacy policy applies to those requests.

Remote configuration. Yapgrab fetches a small signed configuration file from our website, at most every 6 hours. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). It lets us switch off a broken feature, pause or slow down exports of several videos, or show a short notice. No personal data is included in the request. Like any web request, it reveals your IP address and browser user agent to that server. We do not use these to identify you, and we do not keep them beyond standard short-term server logs.

Anonymous failure counter (optional, off by default). If you turn it on, and only when extraction fails, Yapgrab sends the extension version and short failure codes. It never sends video ids, URLs, titles, transcripts, account details or any identifier. Your IP address is visible to the receiving server as with any request, and is not stored with the counts.

Problem reports (only if you choose to send one). The "Report a problem" feature shows you a short text report: the error, which extraction steps failed, the video link, the caption track id, and your extension and browser versions. It never includes transcript text. Yapgrab does not send it. You copy it or open it in your own email app and decide whether to send it to us. If you do, we use it only to look into the problem.

Pro waitlist (only if you sign up)

Yapgrab shows a one-time note about Yapgrab Pro. If you click "Join the waitlist", a form opens in a new tab on Tally (tally.so), a form service. The extension itself sends nothing: you decide whether to fill in and submit the form.

If you submit it, we receive the email address and answers you give (which Pro features interest you, what you use transcripts for, what you would pay, and any comment), plus a marker that you came from the extension. We use them only to tell you when Yapgrab Pro is available and to decide what to build. We do not sell or share them, and we do not use them for advertising. The legal basis is your consent, which you give with the consent checkbox on the form.

Tally is based in Belgium and stores form data in Europe. It processes the data for us as our processor. Its own privacy policy describes its service providers.

We keep your details until Yapgrab Pro launches and we have told you, or until you ask us to delete them, whichever comes first. You can withdraw your consent, unsubscribe, or ask to see, correct or delete your details at any time by contacting us.

What we do not do

We do not use analytics, advertising, fingerprinting or third-party trackers. We do not sell, rent or share data. We do not use data for creditworthiness, lending or any purpose unrelated to Yapgrab's single purpose.

Children

Yapgrab is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them.

Your rights

The extension gives us no personal data about you. If you joined the Pro waitlist, we hold the details you submitted there, and you can ask us to access, correct or delete them. Local data can be removed at any time by removing the extension, or cleared from the hidden debug panel. If you live in the EU or UK you have rights under the GDPR. Contact us with any question.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date and describe the change in the extension's changelog. Material changes, such as any new data collection, will require your opt-in.
