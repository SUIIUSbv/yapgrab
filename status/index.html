Yapgrab says "Extraction failed"?

Video sites change their pages from time to time, and that can break Yapgrab for a while. Try this:

  1. Reload the video page and try again.
  2. Restart Chrome. Chrome updates extensions on its own, and a fix may already be waiting.
  3. Still failing? Click Report a problem in the Yapgrab panel. You see exactly what the report contains, and you send it yourself. It never includes the transcript.

"No captions on this one" is different: that video has no captions, and Yapgrab cannot create them.
